### 1. syntax-cst-lowering (language-pipeline)

**Structure.** parse_with_prelude_enums builds a tree-sitter CST and hands it to one `Lowering<'a>` struct. That struct is a single ~5,700-line impl in ts_parser.rs. It holds the source, the output `declarations`, a RefCell<Vec<ParseError>> error sink, and three pass-1 lookup tables (known_enums, namespace_bindings, entity_bindings), which lower_source_file's first pass fills and nothing writes after that. Pass 2 is a string-keyed `match child.kind()` per container: lower_source_file at top level, lower_member for member kinds, plus separate per-body loops in lower_members, lower_guarded_block, lower_trait_members, lower_purpose_members, lower_specialization_body_members, lower_port_body, lower_connect_body and lower_constraint_def. Lowering an expression takes &self only (lower_expr and what it calls, about lines 4260-5877). There are three fault-handling mechanisms. `check_and_lower!` refuses a subtree before lowering it, via refuse_if_faulty. lower_function_checked runs the inner lowering for its diagnostics, discards the result, and adds a backstop message only if the inner pass said nothing. lower_enum and lower_type_args_from_node report the fault and keep the partial AST. diagnose_error_node, whose CST-walking half lives in the fault_diagnosis.rs satellite, handles direct ERROR children. Member-continuation ambiguity (INV-SF-7 for clean parses) is a separate post-lowering pass: member_continuation.rs walks hand-listed container kinds, and a test-side re-lexer of grammar.js guards that list. Every production consumer (reify-cli, mcp_context, the GUI engine, the LSP's published path, module_dag) returns before compiling when `parsed.errors` is non-empty (documented in reify-compiler compile_builder/pre_pass.rs::forward_parse_errors). So INV-SF-7's safety half reduces to one module-level property: root.has_error() implies errors is non-empty. Nothing enforces that property today. tree-sitter-reify/src/node-types.json, which gives the exact child-kind set of every container, is generated on every build, and no crate reads it.

### 3. realization-pipeline (language-pipeline)

**Structure.** Four entry drivers (build_with_geometry_output, build_snapshot, tessellate_from_values, distance_between_placed) each walk templates and call the receiver-less Engine::execute_realization_ops. That executor short-circuits through probe_realization_cache, or else runs compile_geometry_op → dispatcher::dispatch → an optional conversion recipe → kernel.execute for each op, then terminal VolumeMesh/Voxel conversions. Per-realization results go back to the caller as out-parameters and the small ExecutedRealization struct. Per-op facts about GeometryOp variants have five encodings: the reify-ir GEOMETRY_OP_DESCRIPTORS table (operation, parent_role), exhaustive matches (compiled_geometry_op_to_operation), role-then-inner-OR-match functions with unreachable! tails (parent_handles_for_op, substitute_op_parents), a catch-all match backed by a strum test (classify_op_input_reprs), and per-kind (kind, fn) dispatch tables (`*_COMPILERS`) whose length is locked to a hand-written XKind::ALL in reify-compiler. Engine-scoped state that spans builds: RealizationCache<KernelHandle> (value = bare terminal handle), volume_mesh_sources (meshed-from records, added by #6308 so a cache-served VolumeMesh resolves its egress body), realization_projection_store and morph_source. Per-build state, reset by reset_per_build_state's exhaustive destructure: topology_attribute_table (KernelHandle-keyed), swept_kind_table (bare GeometryHandleId-keyed) and realization_handles (bare id). Within one realization the executor holds lockstep Vec<GeometryHandleId>/Vec<ReprKind> (realization_step_ids/realization_step_reprs). Across realizations it holds named_steps: HashMap<String, KernelHandle> plus a sibling named_step_reprs: HashMap<String, ReprKind>, and module_named_steps snapshots only the handles. After each template, Build and BuildSnapshot run post-processes including redispatch_geometry_consuming_compute_nodes. That function scans every compute node of the graph once per template and uses `realization_inputs.is_empty()` as its 'not yet redispatched' latch. Kernel ownership is carried on KernelHandle through step_handles and named_steps. It is lost at the value boundary (Value::GeometryHandle.kernel_handle: Option<GeometryHandleId>) and in realization_handles/swept_kind_table, and kernel_owner.rs falls back to the default kernel when the owner is not a map key. ### 5. compiler-type-substitution (language-pipeline)

**Structure.** Generic instantiation has one shared Type walker, type_resolution.rs::substitute_type_params, plus its expression twin substitute_expr_result_types. That binding step is re-implemented at about ten sites, each with its own arity policy, message and DiagnosticCode. Sites that build or apply a sigma: expr.rs::instantiate_call_return_type (4 callers since #8232), the empty-collection push-down and match binder_subst inside expr.rs::compile_expr_guarded_with_expected_inner, variant_construct.rs (unify over payloads), compile_builder/entities_phase.rs::check_expr_fn_calls (re-unify), compile_builder/auto_type_param_phase.rs::phase_auto_type_param_resolution (monomorph sigma), type_resolution.rs::normalize_type_guarded (projection), resolve_parameterized_alias and the nested-alias arm of resolve_type_alias_expr_with_subst (TypeExpr-level alias sigma), and entity.rs::check_type_param_bounds (positional effective args). Type expressions are resolved by two full TypeExpr resolvers. resolve_type_expr_with_aliases_kinded resolves direct spellings and resolve_type_alias_expr_with_subst resolves parametric alias bodies. They share only resolve_entity_name_with_shadowing, and since #6477 that includes the applied-form arms. Monomorphization of generic structures exists only inside the auto: resolution phase. It clones the target TopologyTemplate and substitutes a hand-picked subset of its fields. The resolved user-fn call is lowered to CompiledExprKind::UserFunctionCall { function_name, args }, which carries neither the chosen overload nor its type arguments. So the compile-time post-pass (check_expr_fn_calls) and eval (reify-expr find_matching_compiled_function) each re-resolve the overload and re-derive the binding. Key state: the sigma HashMap<String, Type> is always a transient local, never recorded on the IR. The three ambient thread-local resolution scopes are already owned by #8643, and CompilationScope's 30 pub(crate) fields by #8650.

**Diagnosis.** The same decision is coded separately at each site: given a generic's declared type_params (with optional defaults) and the type args written at a use site, is the arity legal, and what sigma results. The policies disagree.
- range [required, total] with defaults filled, uncoded message 'type alias X expects N type argument(s)': type_resolution.rs::resolve_parameterized_alias.
- the same range but a silent `return None`: the nested-alias arm of resolve_type_alias_expr_with_subst.
- strict equality, no defaults, uncoded, degrading to an erased Type::Enum(name): resolve_enum_type_with_args.
- strict equality IGNORING defaults, DiagnosticCode::TypeArgArity: check_applied_type_arg_bounds.
- strict equality, DiagnosticCode::UnresolvedType: the projection arm of normalize_type_guarded.
- 'too many' plus 'missing without default', defaults honoured, uncoded: entity.rs::check_type_param_bounds.
- an explicit-else-default `effective_arg` in phase_auto_type_param_resolution, whose comment says it 'mirrors effective_arg in check_type_param_bounds'.
- an implicit arity 0 that silently drops the args, for builtin, type-param and non-parametric-alias names: resolve_entity_name_with_shadowing's bare-name fallthrough.
- local guards in variant_construct.rs (`args.len() == enum_def.type_params.len()`) and expr.rs's match binder_subst (an unchecked zip).

### 9. constraints-solve (constraints-and-geometry)

**Structure.** reify-eval builds a `ResolutionProblem` (auto params, constraints, dependent cells, objective). It does so in three builders: `build_auto_param_list`, used by `build_solver_problem` and `build_merged_solver_problem`, plus inline copies in `Engine::edit_param` and `Engine::edit_source`. ### 12. elastic-form-find (analysis)

**Structure.** Two kernels share one vocabulary. form_find.rs is the anchored force-density (FD) kernel: form_find_anchored, form_find_anchored_surfaces and form_find_anchored_surfaces_aniso each run validate_members and partition_anchors, assemble a dense D (assemble_d or assemble_d_aniso over scatter_line_members), then solve_reduced, which ends in the is_singular_reduced_solve guard. The two surface entries run a line-identical cotangent Picard loop judged by free_equilibrium_residual_relative. form_find_free.rs is the free-standing kernel. It has two specs: Explicit validates and classifies nullity via validate_explicit, and GroupRatios runs a coordinate-descent eigenvalue search. form_find_free_surfaces is a 311-line outer loop on top of both. It bootstraps from the line-only solve, searches q or fixes q, tries a (2b) null-space trial move, takes a (3) FD-gradient eigen-gap descent step, and applies a stall guard. Its own copies of D assembly, spectrum classification, and residual criterion are private to form_find_free.rs. There are three parallel error enums, FormFindError, AnisoFormFindError and FreeFormError, with a map_ff_error bridge. No validated-problem type exists. The reify-eval trampoline (compute_targets/form_find.rs plus tensegrity_crack) range-checks every index before calling the kernel, so the kernel guards added in #7553 and #6563 are a second, independent copy of the same range check. prestress_stability.rs reuses assemble_force_density_matrix from form_find_free and has its own spectrum code. The stdlib tensegrity.rs accessors decode the same Tensegrity topology a second time, with silent-Undef semantics. Churn in assembly/global.rs is feature work (#8373 node-owned parallel assembly), not bug-driven.
